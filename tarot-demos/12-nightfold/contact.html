I read for people who already know something has to change and want a clearer look at it. Seventeen years, mostly by referral.

FAQ

Do I need to have a question ready?

It helps, but no. Plenty of people arrive with a mess rather than a question, and shaping it is part of the session.

Do you predict the future?

No. I read what is already in motion and where it is likely to lead if nothing changes. That is a different thing, and more useful.

What if I do not like what comes up?

Then we sit with it. I will not soften a reading to keep the session comfortable, and I will not dramatise one to make it memorable.

Can you read about someone who is not present?

Only as far as they affect you. I will not read another person's private life on your behalf.

Is this therapy?

It is not, and it does not replace it. If something surfaces that needs a professional, I will say so plainly.

What happens after I book?

You get a confirmation with a calendar invite and a link. Ten minutes before, you get a reminder. That is the whole process.

Policies

Cancellation. Free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours, the session is held as credit for 90 days.

Disclaimer. Readings are offered for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.
