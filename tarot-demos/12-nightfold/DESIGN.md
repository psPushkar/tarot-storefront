# Aesthetic direction — Nightfold

Purpose: persuade independent international tarot practitioners that a custom booking storefront can feel intimate, serious, and unmistakably theirs while remaining usable for late-night clients ready to book.

Tone: quiet cinematic nocturne, handmade textile map, soft rounded civic wayfinding, never occult costume and never startup gloss.

Constraints: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no image files, fixed source copy, prices in USD first, WCAG-aware contrast, keyboard navigation, one h1 per page, five complete pages, no banned design shortcuts.

Differentiation: the navigation is a sleeping city map. Readings are weather districts stitched into a navy quilt; links sit on deformable street geometry with embroidered lights. Hierarchy comes from thread, spacing, and weather color rather than oversized heavy type. The preloader is the city waking window by window before the quilt unfolds.
