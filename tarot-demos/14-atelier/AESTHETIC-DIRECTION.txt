Purpose: sell a custom booking website to visually literate tarot practitioners while remaining usable for their own clients. Tone: couture atelier lookbook at one-sixth scale — refined, quiet, expensive, and legible rather than theatrical occult. Constraints: plain HTML/CSS/JS, five complete pages, no image files, exact product copy, CSS 3D only, accessible keyboard paths, one authored motion moment. Differentiation: readings become commissioned garments presented as numbered plates; the single red accent marks the chosen look, while ivory, black, blush, and gold behave like box-board, grosgrain, satin, and foil. The first viewport should feel like opening a garment bag to find Aurelia Sable's script label and a real turning dress form, proving the 3D request without scattering motion everywhere.
