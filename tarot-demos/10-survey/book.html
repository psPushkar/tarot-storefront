Booking sequence

  1. Choose a readingFive formats, from a fifteen-minute voice note to a ninety-minute session.
  2. Pick a timeLive availability in your own time zone. No back-and-forth by message.
  3. Pay securelyCard or UPI. You are charged once, at the time of booking.
  4. Receive the readingA calendar invite for live sessions; an email for written and audio ones.
