## The mechanism, scene, cultural home, and what the surface must prove

- **Mechanism:** a structured hour of attention on a question the visitor has been
  circling alone. Not prediction — the cards are the structure.
- **Audience scene:** a working practitioner in Bangalore, evening, laptop,
  deciding whether to pay for a custom site instead of the included theme.
- **Cultural home:** Indian working-document print culture, and the visual world of
  people who consult something before committing to a decision.
- **This surface must prove:** that a site built here makes her practice look like
  expertise worth ₹4,800 for ninety minutes — never a novelty.

## The rut, excluded

- **The rut:** near-black ground, purple and gold, moons, stars, ornate occult
  framing. What every tarot site ships.
- **Its predictable opposite:** cream ground, airy serif, sage accent, spa
  minimalism. What "tasteful tarot" ships to escape the rut.

Both are barred from the list below. Between them they describe two of the demos
already rejected.

## ASSIGNED: candidate 4 — topographic survey sheet

The roll landed on the one I would not have ranked first, which is the point. It also
survives the correction unchanged: the topographic sheet is an international convention,
not an Indian one, so only its framing needed fixing — **not "Survey of India", but the
shared Ordnance Survey / USGS / Swisstopo tradition.**

**Why it survives the truth test:** every relationship it visualises is real. Five
readings genuinely differ in depth and duration — that is elevation. A booking is
genuinely a point located in time — that is a grid reference. The legend genuinely
teaches a notation the visitor then uses. Nothing here requires inventing a
capability the product lacks.

## The contract

- **THESIS** — A reading is a survey of the ground you are already standing on.
  This surface refuses the mystical hero and the airy wellness page alike; it
  presents a practice as a working instrument you learn to read.
- **OWN-WORLD** — Survey sheet: pale drafting ground, contour browns, one
  surveyor's red for the active mark, hairline grid, a legend panel that is real
  navigation rather than decoration. Recognisable with every word removed.
- **STORY** — The visitor arrives circling a decision, sees their situation drawn
  as terrain with gradients and junctions, learns the notation from the legend,
  and locates a reading at a grid reference they choose.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT** — A drawn contour field occupying the full width, the five
  readings sited on it at different elevations, the legend keyed at lower left
  teaching the marks, and the primary action set as a located point, not a button
  floating over a hero.
- **FORM** — Candidate 4 of seven, assigned by seed `600109cd`, built as a survey
  sheet with the legend as navigation.
