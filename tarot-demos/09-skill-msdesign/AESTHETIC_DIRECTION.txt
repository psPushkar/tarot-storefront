Tone: Nocturne editorial atelier — ink, brass, oxidised green, vellum, precise calendar-ledgers; serious, intimate, and unsentimental rather than costume occult.
Constraints: Plain HTML/CSS/JS; no images; CSS-authored grain, meshes and geometry; responsive at 1440/768/375; WCAG 2.1 AA target; CSS-only motion preference with reduced-motion support; exact fixed content only.
Differentiation: A diagonal observatory-ledger composition using coded card-like slabs, orbit rules, and booking-interface craft to make tarot feel like disciplined attention, not Halloween mysticism or startup SaaS.
