Five ways to sit with a questionFive ways to sit with a question

Five formats, from a fifteen-minute voice note to a ninety-minute session.

The Threshold Reading

45 minutes · Live video · $29 · ₹2,400

One question, held properly. For a decision you have been carrying too long.

A focused spread on a single crossroads. You bring one real question — not five — and we spend the whole session on it. Best for a decision with a deadline attached.

Includes

  • A recording you keep
  • Written summary within 24 hours
  • One follow-up question by email

The Full Life Reading

90 minutes · Live video · $58 · ₹4,800

The long look. Work, love, family, money — and how they are pulling against each other.

A wide spread across every area at once, which is the only way to see where one part of your life is quietly paying for another. People usually book this at the start of a year or the end of something.

Includes

  • A recording you keep
  • Written summary within 48 hours
  • A second session at half price within 90 days

The Year Ahead

75 minutes · Live video · $47 · ₹3,900

Twelve cards, twelve months, and the two that actually matter.

A month-by-month spread read as a shape rather than a forecast — where the pressure gathers, where it lifts, and which two months are worth clearing your calendar for.

Includes

  • A recording you keep
  • A one-page year map, illustrated
  • Written summary within 48 hours

The Written Reading

Delivered in 3 days · Written, by email · $22 · ₹1,800

No camera, no calendar. Send the question, receive the reading.

For people in awkward time zones, or who simply think better on paper. You send your question in writing; I read and send back roughly 1,200 words, plus photographs of the spread.

Includes

  • ~1,200 words
  • Photographs of the spread
  • One follow-up question by email

The Voice Note

Delivered in 48 hours · Audio, by email · $11 · ₹900

Fifteen minutes, spoken. The smallest way in.

A short spoken reading on one narrow question. Most people book this first to see whether we get on before spending more.

Includes

  • ~15 minutes of audio
  • Photograph of the spread
